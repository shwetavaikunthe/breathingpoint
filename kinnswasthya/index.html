Everyday Reality

Why Family Care Often Feels Scattered

Instead of juggling paper files, clinic portals, and chat threads, KinnSwasthya brings everyday family health into one connected place.

Records are everywhere

Prescriptions, lab reports, and notes sit across folders, photos, emails, and old chats.

Follow-ups slip easily

Routine check-ins, medicine updates, and recovery steps are easy to miss between visits.

Coordination takes effort

Helping loved ones across homes or cities requires repeated calls and manual updates.

Visits start with hunting

Every consultation begins by searching for prior history, test results, and current medicines.

Sharing lacks clarity

Giving the right family member or provider timely access—with consent—is harder than it should be.

Kinnswasthya Referral Plan — No Separate Joining Required

There is no separate ambassador joining for college students or interns or hospital, clinic, or service provider support staff. Simply register on KinnSwasthya, showcase features to users, patients, and service providers, have them enter your Health ID as the referral code during first-time registration, and earn 11–15% of revenue generated from your referrals monthly.

Track 01 • Care & Clinic Desk

Hospital & Service Provider Support Staff

Built for hospital front-desk executives, clinic coordinators, nursing/pharmacy/lab support teams, and wellness centre staff who interact daily with patients and care providers. No separate sign-up is needed—just register your account and use your Health ID.

  • Promote to Visiting Patients & Providers: Introduce the KinnSwasthya app to patients visiting clinics or service centres and showcase features such as unified family records, AI-assisted report summaries, verified medicine reminders, and consent-based sharing.
  • First-Time Registration With Your Health ID: Ensure every newly onboarded user/patient and service provider enters your Health ID as the referral code during their first-time registration so referrals are tracked to your account.
  • Monthly Referral Payouts: Earn 11–15% of the revenue generated from your referrals paid monthly.
Track 02 • Community & Local Outreach

College Students & Interns

Open to college students and interns who register on KinnSwasthya and share their own Health ID as the referral code—no separate ambassador enrollment required.

  • Apartment Stalls & 1-Hour Community Meetings: Set up small outreach stalls or host structured 1-hour meetings in apartment community halls in your apartment building or neighbourhood to showcase KinnSwasthya features to residents.
  • Onboard Nearby Clinics, Yoga & Physiotherapy Providers: Visit nearby clinics, yoga classes, physiotherapists, and wellness providers to promote KinnSwasthya and help add them as service providers using your Health ID as the referral code during first-time registration.
  • Monthly Referral Payouts: Receive 11–15% of the revenue generated from your referrals paid monthly.
