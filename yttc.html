Yoga Teacher Training Courses

Build a credible teaching career, Levels 1 through 6.

Accredited by the Yoga Certification Board (YCB), Ministry of AYUSH, Government of India — comprehensive training in yoga philosophy, anatomy, teaching methodology and practical technique.

Why train here

Knowledgeable, compassionate, confident yoga professionals

Our YTTC equips aspiring yoga teachers and practitioners with in-depth knowledge across philosophy, anatomy, teaching methodologies and practical technique — enabling graduates to build successful careers while promoting holistic health and well-being.

Every level is internationally relevant and government-accredited, taught by AYUSH-certified evaluators with hands-on therapeutic yoga experience.

Curriculum

Levels 1 – 6

01

Foundation

Introduction to yoga philosophy, basic asana, breath awareness and the yamas & niyamas as a foundation for practice and teaching.

02

Applied Practice

Deepening asana practice with alignment principles, introductory anatomy and physiology relevant to safe teaching.

03

Methodology

Teaching methodology, class sequencing, voice and cueing, and hands-on practicum under supervision.

04

Therapeutic Foundations

Introduction to therapeutic yoga and prop-based adaptation for common physical and stress-related conditions.

05

Advanced Anatomy & Pranayama

Advanced study of anatomy, pranayama techniques and subtle body concepts for experienced practitioners.

06

Evaluator & Consultant Track

Preparation for evaluator-level certification and therapeutic yoga consultancy, aligned with YCB standards.

Level-wise duration, fees and intake dates are shared during enquiry — course structure aligns with Yoga Certification Board, Ministry of AYUSH guidelines.
