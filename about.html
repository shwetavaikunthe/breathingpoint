    About Breathing Point

    Where traditional yoga meets therapeutic care.

    Breathing Point Integrative Therapy offers a holistic approach to wellness by integrating traditional yoga practices with therapeutic yoga using specialised props to support emotional, mental and physical well-being.

    Our Vision

    A trusted center for integrative wellness

    To be a trusted center for integrative wellness by transforming lives through the harmonious integration of therapeutic yoga, breathwork, mindful living and holistic healing.

    We envision empowering individuals, families and communities to achieve lifelong physical vitality, mental clarity, emotional resilience and spiritual well-being — while nurturing the next generation of certified yoga professionals.

    Our Mission

    Inspiring healthier lives through personalised, evidence-informed practice

    At Breathing Point Integrative Therapy, our mission is to inspire healthier lives through personalised, evidence-informed holistic wellness programs that combine traditional yoga, therapeutic yoga with specialised props, breathwork, mindfulness and integrative healing practices.

    Safe & Inclusive Care

    Delivering personalized wellness solutions for people of all ages and abilities.

    Preventive Healthcare

    Enhancing physical, mental, emotional and spiritual well-being.

    Excellence in Education

    YCB, Ministry of AYUSH-certified Yoga Teacher Training, Levels 1–6, developing knowledgeable, compassionate, confident professionals.

    Community & Growth

    Building a supportive space for lifelong learning, self-awareness and sustainable healthy living.

    Integrative Approach

    Blending the wisdom of traditional yoga with modern therapeutic approaches to create meaningful, lasting transformation for individuals and society.
