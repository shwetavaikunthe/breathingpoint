Therapeutic Wellness Programs

Yoga, Pranayam & Meditation — tailored to your body's needs.

Personalised programs designed to improve flexibility, strength, posture and stress management, using specialised therapeutic props — for individuals of all ages and abilities.

The approach

Traditional yoga, supported by therapeutic props

Where a general class asks every body to move the same way, therapeutic yoga adapts the pose to the person — using bolsters, blocks, straps, chairs and walls so that each posture is accessible, safe and genuinely beneficial, whatever your starting point.

Sessions are built around you: your history, your goals, and the specific areas — physical, mental or emotional — you want to strengthen.

Focus areas

What our programs support

Flexibility & Strength

Pranayam & Breathwork

Quality of Life

Sustainable, whole-person routines that fit real life — for all ages and abilities.
